CRYSTAL WATERS PLUMBING &#038; DRAINAGE
## WE ARE YOUR LOCAL COMMUNITY PLUMBING &#038; DRAINAGE SPECIALIST.
Established July 2018, Crystal Waters Plumbing & Drainage was named after the spectacular crystal clear waterfalls that cascade down & carve their way through the towering rainforest, tumbling down the creeks before snaking their way out to the Pacific ocean. Our work is carried out to an extremely high standard and customer service is of the utmost importance. With over 25 years experience in the plumbing industry, we fix leaky tap washers to new homes and everything in between.  Burst pipe?…we got you covered. Gas leak?…we got that covered too.
If you need an electrician, check out our sister company, Crystal Waters Electrical and talk to Darren on 0402555032 who carries out some of the finest work on the Gold Coast

## Water Leaks
At Crystal Waters Plumbing & Drainage, we specialize in the detection and remediation of both internal and external water leaks.  Equipped with advanced tools and extensive experience, our team is dedicated to providing thorough and efficient leak detection.
We employ a meticulous approach to pinpoint and resolve leaks promptly.  With a commitment to professionalism and precision, we ensure that every leak is identified and addressed with the utmost care, safeguarding your property from potential damage and inconvenience.  Trust in our expertise to deliver reliable solutions for all your plumbing needs.

DRONE FOOTAGE SPECIALISTS
## WATER LEAKS FROM ABOVE.
Crystal Waters Plumbing & Drainage offers a sophisticated solution to accessing challenging spaces through our drone footage services.  Employing cutting-edge drone technology equipped with high-resolution cameras, we provide a comprehensive visual perspective of areas traditionally difficult to reach.  From towering structures to complex industrial environments, our drone navigates with precision, capturing detailed footage for thorough inspection and assessment of possible water ingress.  This method not only mitigates safety risks associated with manual inspections but also ensures meticulous documentation for informed decision-making and strategic planning.  Trust in our professional drone services to deliver efficient and effective solutions for your inspection needs.
BURST PIPE SPECIALISTS
## WATER LEAKS FROM BELOW.
Our burst pipe location and repair service is designed to minimize damage and restore peace of mind in record time. Using advanced technology and expert techniques, we swiftly pinpoint the source of the leak. Once located, our skilled technicians leap into action, swiftly repairing the burst pipe with precision and care.
With our efficient and effective approach, you can trust that your property is in capable hands, and we'll have your plumbing back in working order before you know it.
THERMAL IMAGING TECHNOLOGY
## HIDDEN INTERNAL WATER LEAKS.
With our state-of-the-art thermal imaging technology, we offer a pioneering approach to detecting internal water leaks with unmatched precision.  Harnessing the power of infrared thermography, our cutting-edge equipment translates subtle temperature variations into vivid visual representations, revealing hidden leaks with exceptional clarity.  Whether concealed behind walls or nestled within flooring, our thermal imaging system effortlessly identifies the sources of moisture, enabling swift and accurate diagnosis
Trust in our expertise and innovative tools to deliver reliable solutions for identifying and addressing internal water leaks effectively.
